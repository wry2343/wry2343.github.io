Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2 October 2026

Application

This policy applies to Hermes Finance Tracker, a private personal-finance tool used by Codey Reece.

Google data accessed

The application requests the Gmail read-only scope. It reads selected messages containing Discovery Bank transaction alerts and supported grocery receipts. It cannot send, delete, edit or move email.

How data is used

Message information is used only to maintain the user's private local transaction and receipt records and produce personal budget summaries.

Storage and sharing

Processed records are stored locally on the user's Mac. Data is not sold, used for advertising, or shared with unrelated third parties. No finance records are stored on this public website.

Retention and deletion

The user controls the locally stored records and may delete them at any time. Google access may be revoked at any time through the user's Google Account security settings.

Limited use

The application's use of information received from Google APIs is limited to providing and improving the user-facing finance-tracking functionality described above.

Contact

Questions may be sent to codeyg43@gmail.com.
